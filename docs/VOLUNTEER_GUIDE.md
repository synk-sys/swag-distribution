# Swag counter: volunteer guide

**Rule: one swag bag per registered attendee. The app decides, not you.**

## Setup (2 minutes)

1. Open the app link on your phone and sign in.
2. Choose your counter, for example **Counter 1**.
3. Allow camera access. Check that the badge at the top says **Online** in green.
4. Tap the speaker icon if you want sounds. Phones also vibrate.

## For each attendee

1. Ask for their **Bevy ticket barcode** (in the Bevy email or app, or on their badge) and hold it inside the white frame.
2. Read the colour:

| Screen | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 🟩 **ELIGIBLE** | Check that the name looks right. Tap **Confirm Distributed**. **Wait for RECORDED ✓**, then hand over **one** bag. |
| 🟥 **ALREADY COLLECTED** | **Do not give a bag.** The screen shows when and where they collected it. Send disputes to an organizer. |
| 🟨 **NOT FOUND** | Tap **Manual lookup**, search by name, email or ticket number, and check their badge or ID. |
| ⬜ **INELIGIBLE / OUT OF STOCK** | No bag. Send them to an organizer if they disagree. |
| 🟨 **NOT CONFIRMED** | The network dropped. **Don't hand over a bag yet.** Tap **Retry**. Retrying is safe and can't double-count. |

3. After RECORDED ✓, the scanner is ready again automatically after 2–3 seconds. You can also tap the screen.

## Good to know

- If two of you scan the same person at the same time, only one of you gets RECORDED ✓. The other sees ALREADY COLLECTED.
- If the badge at the top turns red (**OFFLINE**), stop handing out bags. Confirm is disabled until the connection returns.
- You **cannot** undo a claim. If you made a mistake, for example confirmed the wrong person, tell an organizer right away.
- If an organizer has turned on name checks, tick **Name matches badge / ID** before confirming.
- If the camera won't start, type the barcode (e.g. `132774:3469041`) or use manual lookup. On iPhone use Safari, and on Android use Chrome.
